# /consulting surface brief

Scope: Persuade. One route, `src/pages/consulting.tsx`, built to replace `/` later. Audience: startup founders and engineering leaders deciding whether to book a 30-min call. Action: book (cal.com). Proof: Portico and Atomic. Constraints: copy frozen, green-only, light+dark, tiered motion, <= 60 KB new JS. Full spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-consulting-landing-impact-design.md

## Direction contract

THESIS: The release line. Shipping AI to production is a controlled release, disorder in and one clean line out, and the page is that line. Refuses the AI-consulting default: glowing orb or link-constellation particles, purple gradients, a generic stat-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Green-only ink on theme-flipped Infima neutrals; 1px oriented ticks and a single 2px signal line with one bright pilot dot; Besley slab display against the system sans; two drenched green bands (stats, close); resting depth only on proof screenshot frames. Recognizable with content removed: a field of ticks aligning into a line.

STORY: The visitor watches disorder resolve into one clean line, understands "pilot to production without breakage", follows the line through three gates, sees named proof arrive, and books a 30-minute call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440x900. Headline left (Besley, two lines, 5-6rem) with subhead, primary CTA, microcopy; full-bleed tick field behind it, quiet behind the text, aligning into a line that passes behind Fadee's portrait tile on the right; the stats band peeks at the bottom edge.

FORM: Code-led. Signature interaction: pointer-disturbed tick field that settles into the release line, handing off to the scroll-scrubbed RolloutRail and returning settled behind the final CTA. Position on list: user-pinned (concept-seed not run). Seed key: none (user-pinned direction).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
