# Vertical Software Summit field plan

The [Vertical Software Summit](https://verticalsaasgroup.com/conference) is in Miami on November 4-5, 2026, with an opening party on November 3. It concentrates vertical SaaS founders, operators, and investors, making it a targeted source of prospective clients and referral partners.

## Pre-event: September-November 2

- Build a priority list of 30-40 people: founders/operators in relevant verticals, speakers, and investors with potentially relevant portfolios.
- Identify 15-20 people worth a personalized pre-event note.
- Book 5-8 short conversations around the opening party, networking breaks, meals, or before/after programming.
- Prepare a simple digital card or landing-page link with your one-sentence position and fit-call CTA.

### Pre-event meetup note

> Hi [Name] - I will be at the Vertical Software Summit in Miami and saw [specific company, session, or vertical]. I help SaaS engineering teams use AI to accelerate delivery without destabilizing production systems. I would enjoy hearing how you are thinking about that transition at [Company]. Are you open to a 15-minute coffee during the Summit?

## Onsite: November 3-5

- Prioritize the opening party, networking breaks, meals, and closing party. Use sessions to learn; use the networking windows to meet people.
- Ask about delivery bottlenecks, AI pilots that did not reach production, legacy constraints, reliability risks, and roadmap pressure.
- Do not pitch before there is a relevant problem. Ask smart questions and offer a follow-up only when it is genuinely useful.
- After every valuable conversation, record the person, company, pain signal, promised next step, and deadline.

### Conference scorecard

- 12 meaningful conversations.
- 5 strong follow-up contacts.
- 2-3 post-event fit calls.

Investor conversations count when the investor can credibly refer a portfolio company or operator with a relevant need.

## Follow-up: November 6-13

- Send a personal follow-up within 24 hours and reference the actual conversation.
- Offer a fit call within the following week.
- After a reply, share a concise point of view or case study if it is relevant.

### Post-event follow-up

> Hi [Name] - it was great meeting you at the Vertical Software Summit. I kept thinking about what you said regarding [specific issue]. The combination of [delivery/reliability/AI constraint] sounds like the kind of problem where a short assessment could clarify the fastest safe path forward. Would you be open to a 30-minute follow-up next week?
