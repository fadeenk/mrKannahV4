# Strategy and offers

## Commercial position

> Kannah Consulting helps SaaS engineering teams introduce AI into their development workflow and production systems so they ship faster without sacrificing reliability, security, or engineering ownership.

Lead with this outcome, not a list of technologies or every service you can provide.

## Target buyers

- CTOs, VPs/Heads of Engineering, and technical founders who own delivery outcomes.
- Established SaaS companies with an existing system, active roadmap, and real engineering constraints.
- Companies in verticals such as education, construction, and insurance, particularly where workflows are complex, regulated, or difficult to modernize.
- Investors and operators only when they can refer relevant portfolio companies or founders.

## Offer A: AI Delivery & Reliability Assessment

A paid, fixed-scope 1-2 week engagement that gives a technical leader a practical path forward.

Deliverables:

- Review of delivery workflow, bottlenecks, production risks, and AI opportunities.
- A prioritized implementation roadmap with effort, sequencing, and risk notes.
- A small proof of value when useful and safe.

Suggested starting range: **$4,000-$7,500**. Increase the price for larger systems, multiple stakeholder groups, or deeper technical review.

## Offer B: Embedded AI Delivery Partner

A three-month minimum retainer in which you own a defined codebase area or delivery workstream.

Responsibilities:

- Implement AI-enabled workflows or product capabilities in production.
- Improve delivery speed, testing, stability, and reliability.
- Work directly with the existing engineering team as an accountable owner, not a presentation-only adviser.

Starting floor: **10-12 hours/week at $170/hour**. Define scope, ownership, access, cadence, and success measures in writing.

## Conversion rule

When urgency, scope, and ownership are clear, offer the retainer directly. When a buyer needs confidence about the problem or approach first, offer the paid assessment. Do not give away a full diagnosis on a free call.

## Do not lead with

- Generic web development or generic “AI consulting.”
- A broad catalog of your capabilities.
- A new micro-SaaS idea before a customer has described the problem and willingness to pay.
- A hard sales pitch in a first message; the first objective is a relevant conversation.
