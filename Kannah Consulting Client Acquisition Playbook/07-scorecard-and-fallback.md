# Scorecard and fallback plan

## Leading indicators to protect

- **Outbound consistency:** 20-25 high-quality new messages each week.
- **Conversations:** at least two qualified calls per week once outreach gains momentum.
- **Speed:** reply to qualified leads within one business day; send proposals within 48 hours.
- **Proof:** publish one useful insight per week and one deeper case study per month.

## Part-time fallback

If the October 31 gate is missed, start a parallel search for a part-time senior engineering, AI implementation, or fractional technical-leadership contract.

Keep the consulting system running. Block a fixed weekly time allocation for the job search rather than giving it all available time. The role should stabilize revenue without hiding the core consulting offer.

## Rules that protect time and reputation

- Do not disclose confidential client information or use client code as public portfolio proof.
- Use written scope, payment terms, ownership boundaries, and access expectations before beginning paid work.
- Do not lower price to compensate for unclear scope. Narrow the initial engagement instead.
- Keep delivery capacity below the point where sales follow-up or work quality collapse.

## Start-this-week checklist

- Draft the focused consulting landing page.
- Choose the first two anonymized case studies.
- Send the first five warm-reconnection messages.
- Set up the pipeline tracker and next Friday’s review.
- Reserve two call blocks on your calendar.

Do not wait for the landing page to be perfect before starting conversations.
