# Weekly operating system

Run this system every week until the revenue target is secured. Treat sales time as planned client-delivery work, not optional homework.

## Weekly minimums

- Send 20-25 thoughtful outbound messages to named SaaS leaders.
- Follow up with warm contacts, active leads, and referral sources.
- Publish one LinkedIn post based on a real lesson from AI-enabled delivery, reliability, or technical leadership.
- Hold two calendar blocks open for discovery calls and proposal work.
- Complete a 20-minute Friday pipeline review.

## Suggested time blocks while the current engagement is active

- **Monday:** 90 minutes for account research and tailored messages.
- **Tuesday and Thursday:** 45 minutes each for warm follow-ups and relevant LinkedIn engagement.
- **Wednesday:** 90 minutes for a proof asset, case study, or LinkedIn post.
- **Friday:** 60 minutes for pipeline review, proposals, and next-week planning.
- **Calls:** reserve two 60-minute blocks each week. Reschedule them when necessary, but do not remove them.

## Pipeline record for every lead

Track the following in one place:

- Name, company, role, source, vertical, and a public profile/company link.
- Problem signal, last-contact date, agreed next step, next-step date, and estimated value.
- Stage: `target`, `contacted`, `replied`, `call booked`, `qualified`, `assessment proposed`, `retainer proposed`, `won`, or `closed-lost`.

## Friday review questions

- How many warm messages, targeted messages, and follow-ups did I send?
- How many replies, calls, qualified opportunities, proposals, paid assessments, and signed retainers did they produce?
- Which vertical, role, message, or referral source generated the strongest signal?
- Does every active opportunity have a concrete next action and date?
