# 90-day calendar

## August 28-September 4: launch

Outcome: a clear message and the first relevant conversations.

- Create one focused consulting landing page.
- Prepare two anonymized case studies.
- Send 5-10 personal warm-network messages.
- Build an initial list of 25 relevant SaaS engineering leaders.

## September 5-October 1: validate

Outcome: 6-10 real conversations and 1-2 proposals.

- Send 20-25 tailored outbound messages each week.
- Publish one proof-led LinkedIn post each week.
- Run fit calls and propose an assessment or retainer based on the buyer’s needs.

## October 2-November 2: prepare and advance

Outcome: a qualified conference pipeline and active proposals.

- Build the priority list for the Vertical Software Summit.
- Send 15-20 pre-event messages and book 5-8 in-person conversations.
- Continue normal follow-up and proposal work; the conference supplements outreach rather than pausing it.

## November 3-5: convert at the conference

Outcome: 12 meaningful conversations, 5 strong follow-up contacts, and 2-3 post-event fit calls.

Follow the detailed [Vertical Software Summit field plan](./06-vertical-software-summit.md).

## November 6-27: close and stabilize

Outcome: two retainer opportunities at the right stage to create recurring revenue.

- Follow up with conference contacts within 24 hours.
- Run follow-up calls within one week.
- Send proposals within 48 hours of a strong call.
- Convert strong early work into anonymized proof.

## November 28-December 31: make revenue repeatable

- Ask clients for referrals and testimonials.
- Document the strongest proof points from winning engagements.
- Expand retainers where justified.
- Use the part-time fallback only if the relevant decision gate was missed.

## Decision gates

### September 30

If there are fewer than six qualified conversations, revise the target audience and first message before adding more outreach volume. Double down on warm introductions.

### October 31

If there is no paid assessment or signed retainer, begin a parallel search for a part-time senior engineering, AI implementation, or fractional technical-leadership contract while maintaining the consulting system.

### November 27

Target two 12-hour/week retainers, or an equivalent combination producing at least $15,000/month in committed revenue.
